good to know · everything here is temporary
  • There's no account and no password. Your profile only exists while you're online.
  • When you log off, close or reload this page, or lose your connection for more than 5 minutes, your profile, requests and chats are deleted for good. Nobody can bring them back, not even us.
  • After 30 minutes without doing anything, you're logged off automatically.
  • Next time, you start from scratch with a new profile. Earlier chats can't be continued.
  • While you're online, anyone online can see your profile. People can only reach you with a request and a message, and you decide who gets through.
